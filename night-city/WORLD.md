# Wick — World Bible

Wick is a city that exists only between dusk and dawn. It is not hidden; it is
simply not there during the day. Visitors arrive by being awake at the right
hour. What they find depends on their real local time and the real weather
above them.

## Rules of the city
1. Wick opens at 7 PM local time and is gone by 6 AM. During the day, the site
   shows an empty lot and a countdown.
2. Every hour band shows a different face of the same street.
3. Rain floods the low quarter and opens the **Drowned Market**.
4. Fog moves things around. Street signs can't be trusted in fog.
5. Every scene hides one detail that points to another hour or weather. That's
   what brings people back.
6. Nothing in Wick is cruel. It is strange, tender, and a little sad.

## Geography
- **Tallow Street**: the main street. It runs downhill from the Lamp Stair to
  the canal.
- **The Lamp Stair**: 212 steps. The count changes in fog.
- **The Sill**: a black canal at the bottom of Tallow Street. The night ferry
  crosses it.
- **The Drowned Market**: a sunken square. It exists only when it is raining
  somewhere above the visitor.
- **The Tower**: a radio mast on the far bank. It broadcasts static.

## Hour bands
| Band | Local time | Mood |
|---|---|---|
| Lamplight | 7:00 PM – 9:59 PM | Opening. Shops unshutter. Hopeful. |
| The Long Hour | 10:00 PM – 12:59 AM | Busy, warm, and a little uncanny. |
| Lowtide | 1:00 AM – 3:59 AM | Few people. The strangest offices open. |
| Thinning | 4:00 AM – 5:59 AM | The city starts to fold up. Goodbyes. |

## Recurring characters
- **Ottoline Marsh**: the lamplighter. She lights lamps that are already lit,
  "to remind them".
- **Mr. Pell**: a cartographer. He sells maps of streets that close at dawn.
  He is always slightly out of date.
- **Halden**: a baker. He bakes bread for people who haven't arrived yet.
- **The Clerk**: runs the Lost Property Office, which holds things people lost
  in dreams. It is open only at Lowtide.
- **Tuesday**: a grey cat who belongs to nobody and leads people to places.
- **The Ferryman**: never speaks. He takes payment in the last thing you said
  out loud.

## Recurring objects
- Brass lamp keys
- Maps with wet edges
- Ferry tokens stamped with a different hour on each side
- Static from the Tower that sounds, briefly, like your name

## Expansion ideas (later versions)
- New streets that unlock after you've seen all 12 Tallow Street scenes
- Moon phase effects (a full moon opens the Tower)
- Special editions for holidays and solstices
- Rare snow scenes
- A guestbook in the Lost Property Office where visitors leave one "lost thing"
